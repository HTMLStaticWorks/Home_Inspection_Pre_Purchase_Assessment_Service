Executive Summary

Major Defect: Electrical Panel

Action Required
5.1 Main Electrical Panel - Double Tapped Breakers

Two or more wires were observed terminating on a single breaker lug (double tapping) in the main service panel. Most breakers are designed for only one wire. This condition can cause loose connections, arcing, and poses a potential fire hazard.

Recommendation: A licensed electrician should evaluate and properly correct this condition.

Maintenance: Roofing

Monitor / Repair
2.3 Roof Coverings - Missing/Damaged Shingles

Several architectural asphalt shingles on the rear slope exhibit damage or are missing. While no active leaks were detected in the attic space, this compromises the weather-tightness of the roof envelope.

Recommendation: Have a qualified roofing contractor replace missing or damaged shingles to prevent future water intrusion.
