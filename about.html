About ApexInspect

Driven by a passion for property safety, accuracy, and client protection. We bring international standards to every inspection we perform.

Our Story

Founded in 2010, ApexInspect was built on a simple premise: homebuyers deserve to know exactly what they are investing in. Too often, superficial walkthroughs miss critical issues that cost families thousands.

We revolutionized the local market by introducing comprehensive, technology-driven inspections. Using thermal imaging, drone technology, and advanced reporting software, we set a new standard for accuracy.

Today, our team of certified professionals has protected over 5,000 investments, earning the trust of homebuyers, sellers, and real estate professionals alike.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking your home inspection.

Most standard home inspections take between 2–4 hours, depending on the size and age of the property. Larger homes or those with additional systems like pools or detached structures may take longer.

We strongly encourage buyers to attend the inspection. Walking through the property with our inspector gives you firsthand knowledge about any issues discovered and a chance to ask questions in real time.

Your comprehensive digital report — complete with photos, annotations, and priority ratings — will be delivered to your secure client portal within 24 hours of the inspection completion.

A standard inspection covers all visually accessible areas. Items behind walls, underground pipes, and certain specialist areas like asbestos or mold testing may require separate specialist inspections, which we can also arrange.

Simply fill out our contact form or call us at (555) 123-4567. We'll confirm your preferred date and time, and send a booking confirmation within the hour.
