Thermal Infrared Moisture & Insulation Scan

Sellers often apply a fresh coat of paint to conceal water stains and slow ceiling leaks before an open house. Our calibrated FLIR thermal imaging cameras detect surface temperature variances indicative of active moisture behind sheetrock, missing fiberglass insulation in exterior walls, and hidden radiant heat loss.

  • ✓

Mechanical & Life Safety

Electrical Panel & HVAC Safety Diagnostic

Electrical and combustion heating failures represent the leading causes of residential property fires and accidental carbon monoxide poisoning. Our comprehensive mechanical diagnostic removes electrical panel dead fronts to verify proper breaker sizing, neutral wire isolation, and tests gas furnace chambers for hazardous cracked exchangers.

Fire Hazard Recalls: Uncovers obsolete Federal Pacific Stab-Lok and Zinsco panels that insurers refuse to cover.
Ground & Arc Fault (AFCI/GFCI): Tests receptacle safety in bathrooms, kitchens, and outdoor circuits.
Combustion Gas & CO Testing: Calibrated electronic sniffers test furnace burner plenums for life-threatening leaks.

Environmental Health Diagnostics

Mold, Radon & Indoor Air Quality Testing

Radon gas is an invisible, odorless radioactive byproduct of decaying uranium in bedrock and represents the second leading cause of lung cancer nationwide. Combined with black mold (Stachybotrys) spore growth stemming from hidden leaks, poor air quality compromises family health before symptoms appear.

48-Hr Digital Radon Test

Continuous hourly air monitoring calibrated to EPA action thresholds (4.0 pCi/L).

EPA Accredited Lab Results

Air-o-Cell cassettes analyzed by certified third-party mycologists for spore concentration.

Indoor vs. Outdoor Baseline

Contrasts interior ambient spore loads with natural outdoor background air controls.

Mitigation Cost Roadmap
