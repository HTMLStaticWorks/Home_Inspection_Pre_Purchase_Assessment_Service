What We Do

Independent Pre-Purchase Evaluations Dedicated Solely to Buyers

ApexInspect works strictly for you, the property buyer. We conduct meticulous, non-invasive physical and technical evaluations of residential properties during your contract contingency window, delivering deep insight before you finalize your closing documents.

VS

Technical, Structural & Safety Assessments vs. Surface Cosmetics

A certified home inspector's role is fundamentally different from a casual walkthrough or interior decorating appraisal. We do not focus on cosmetic details such as wall paint, carpet colors, or staged furniture. Instead, we perform rigorous diagnostic evaluations of the property's technical systems, load-bearing structures, and occupant life safety conditions.

What We Assess (Structural, Technical & Safety)

  • • Structural Stability: Foundation settling, load-bearing walls, roof framing trusses, and subfloor deflection.
  • • Technical Systems: Main breaker panels, electrical grounding, HVAC furnaces, water heaters, and drain lines.
  • • Life Safety Conditions: Fire hazard panels, carbon monoxide leaks, gas supply safety, and hidden mold intrusion.

What We Do NOT Focus On (Surface Cosmetics)

  • • Paint & Wallpaper: Fresh paint colors, aesthetic wall scuffs, or decorative wallpaper selections.
  • • Staging Furniture: Interior decorator furniture, window drapes, rugs, or decorative design tastes.
  • • Superficial Wear: Minor cosmetic scratches that do not impact structural integrity or personal safety.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking your home inspection.

Most standard home inspections take between 2–4 hours, depending on the size and age of the property. Larger homes or those with additional systems like pools or detached structures may take longer.

We strongly encourage buyers to attend the inspection. Walking through the property with our inspector gives you firsthand knowledge about any issues discovered and a chance to ask questions in real time.

Your comprehensive digital report — complete with photos, annotations, and priority ratings — will be delivered to your secure client portal within 24 hours of the inspection completion.

A standard inspection covers all visually accessible areas. Items behind walls, underground pipes, and certain specialist areas like asbestos or mold testing may require separate specialist inspections, which we can also arrange.
